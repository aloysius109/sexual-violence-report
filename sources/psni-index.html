Homicides recorded by the police in Northern Ireland, latest figures as of 10th September 2026

This bulletin is updated following each homicide recorded by the police in Northern Ireland.  Figures are provided on a financial year basis from April 2007 and a calendar year basis from January 2008. Figures will only be published Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays.

Outcomes of Crimes Recorded by the Police in Northern Ireland 2015/16 to 2024/25 (published 14 May 2026)

This bulletin presents the wider outcomes framework within PSNI, summarising the outcomes that have been assigned to crimes recorded by the police between 2015/16 and 2024/25. This bulletin can be read alongside the latest trends bulletin in order to see the wider context of recorded crime and crime outcomes.

  • Police Crime Outcomes in Northern Ireland 2015 16 to 2024 25 (PDF, 791.84 KB)

Agriculture and Rural Crime in Northern Ireland

This bulletin presents the number of crimes of burglary, robbery and theft recorded by the police that are linked to agricultural activity and provides a breakdown of burglary, robbery and theft by urban/rural settlement areas.
